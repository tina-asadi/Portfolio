FAQ's

Quick answers

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Send us a message and we’ll help.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 1 business day. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days and is free on orders over $500.

What is your return policy?

You can return any watch or band within 30 days in its original condition for a full refund.

Do bands fit every watch?

Our sport bands fit 41–45 mm cases. Check the product details or ask a store team member if you're not sure.

How does trade-in work?

Bring an eligible device to any store or start online. We'll give you an instant quote and apply the credit to your new watch.

Is there a warranty?

Every watch comes with a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. Bands are covered for 1 year.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Choose Card Monthly Installments at checkout to pay at 0% APR over 6 or 12 months.
