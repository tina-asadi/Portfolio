A short guide

How to read a Persian carpet

Most city carpets share the same architecture. Once you know the parts and their Persian names, you can describe any piece you see in a showroom.

    The process

    From fleece to floor

    A room-size city carpet takes months to finish. These are the stages every Golnar piece passes through, in order.

    1. 1

      Spinning

      ریسندگی

      Fleece from Kork and highland sheep is washed and spun by hand. Small changes in thickness give the pile its soft, living sheen.

    2. 2

      Dyeing

      رنگرزی

      Yarn is dyed in small batches with roots, rinds and leaves. Each batch differs slightly, and the faint bands this leaves are called abrash.

    3. 3

      Knotting

      بافت

      Each knot is tied around two warp threads and cut with a hook knife. Most workshops tie the asymmetric senneh knot; Tabriz and Heriz often use the symmetric one.

    4. 4

      Washing

      شستشو

      The finished carpet is washed and laid flat in the sun so the pile and the dyes settle.

    5. 5

      Clipping

      پرداخت

      The pile is sheared to an even height. Fine city carpets are clipped short to keep the drawing crisp.
